# Figure note

`central_beijing_100_fixed_road_network.png` is the reviewed 3000x3000 paper
figure (SHA-256
`e186d99e005d4ce4c366cde4039e459c035028f00383ca98eb02904f6caeade4`).

The light, medium and dark road lines show local, middle-class and major road
classes, respectively. Their shade/width is only background map styling. It
does not encode graph weight, traffic, uncertainty, a fitted parameter or a
simulation result. The thin gray qNN edges connect selected nodes for the
statistical graph; they are not drawn shortest-road routes. Colored points show
the five equal-size road-distance groups.
