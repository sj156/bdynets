Downstream forecasts after an upstream surge

The source experiences repeated unannounced surges. The target may have independent demand changes and may respond to the previous observed source count. Its first usable downstream forecast follows the observed upstream onset by one bin.

Run scope. Profile smoke, 4 generated scenario(s), 1 distinct replicate ID(s). This execution check uses reduced dimensions or sampling budgets. Its scores cannot establish method rankings.

Reproduce from the repository root

Set RESULTS to the external results directory. The generator stores a panel and its identity. The fit driver reads the panel and records a job by method. The comparison driver collects scores, numbers of matched replicates, Monte Carlo errors, and incomplete jobs. Select another cell with --scenario or an individual fit with --method.

CASE=GLS-paper/experiments/03-surges
Rscript "$CASE/generate.R" --output="$RESULTS" --profile=smoke --scenario=local-on-transmit-on --replicate=1
Rscript "$CASE/fit.R" --output="$RESULTS" --profile=smoke --scenario=local-on-transmit-on --replicate=1 --method=all
Rscript "$CASE/compare.R" --output="$RESULTS" --profile=smoke
Rscript GLS-paper/reports/render.R 03-surges "$RESULTS/03-surges"

The full results directory contains the individual job statuses, all metrics in comparison.csv, and replicate-level paired differences in paired-replicates.csv. Rendering reads those saved results.
