When the supplied graph contains wrong links

The published PNAR control uses a known network average and a separate own lag. The experiment measures forecast error on the same count panel as increasing fractions of the supplied directed edges are replaced. The full and restricted GLS fits show the effect of admitting predictors outside the supplied graph.

Run scope. Profile smoke, 6 generated scenario(s), 1 distinct replicate ID(s). This execution check uses reduced dimensions or sampling budgets. Its scores cannot establish method rankings.

Reproduce from the repository root

Set RESULTS to the external results directory. The generator stores a panel and its identity. The fit driver reads the panel and records a job by method. The comparison driver collects scores, numbers of matched replicates, Monte Carlo errors, and incomplete jobs. Select another cell with --scenario or an individual fit with --method.

CASE=GLS-paper/experiments/01-graph-error
Rscript "$CASE/generate.R" --output="$RESULTS" --profile=smoke --scenario=T100-error25 --replicate=1
Rscript "$CASE/fit.R" --output="$RESULTS" --profile=smoke --scenario=T100-error25 --replicate=1 --method=all
Rscript "$CASE/compare.R" --output="$RESULTS" --profile=smoke
Rscript GLS-paper/reports/render.R 01-graph-error "$RESULTS/01-graph-error"

The full results directory contains the individual job statuses, all metrics in comparison.csv, and replicate-level paired differences in paired-replicates.csv. Rendering reads those saved results.
