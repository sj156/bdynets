Data and indicators

Hourly station flow sums available five-minute total-flow records. Each displayed road connects adjacent stations on the same freeway and direction; its flow is the mean of available endpoint station values.

Zero is valid. Gray links have no available endpoint value. The source-clock labels have an unspecified measurement timezone.

The dataset has 24 hourly rows and 4,435 display links. Basemap roads, water and labels come from the packaged offline OSM extract.
